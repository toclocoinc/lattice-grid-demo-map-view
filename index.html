Map View: a fibre build schedule in Colchester, as a 3D map

A synthetic schedule: about 2,000 premises (columns, as tall as the premises they pass), 20 build zones and 30 duct routes with flow, coloured by status, over OpenFreeMap with 3D buildings. Filter, search or group the grid and the map follows; select a row and the map flies to it; click a column and the grid selects and scrolls to its row.

Synthetic data, seeded (no real address, premises or route). Basemap: OpenFreeMap Positron, © OpenMapTiles, data © OpenStreetMap contributors. Drawing: deck.gl and MapLibre GL JS. Built with Lattice Grid 1.84.0 (modules/mapview). Source.
